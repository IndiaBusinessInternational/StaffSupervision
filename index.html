always works on one machine, so recording it gives you context on the card. It is not proof of who did what — no IBI app records the device that made a change.

Staff laptops can't be read over the Wi-Fi (client isolation is on), so run this on that laptop and copy the four values in:

$a = Get-NetAdapter | Where-Object Status -eq 'Up' | Select-Object -First 1 [PSCustomObject]@{ Hostname=$env:COMPUTERNAME; MAC=$a.MacAddress; Model=(Get-CimInstance Win32_ComputerSystem).Model; Serial=(Get-CimInstance Win32_BIOS).SerialNumber }
